Performance commentary
Based on transaction prices, the fund's return was -0.11%.
The 3D Emerging Equity UCITS ETF aims to consistently outperform the MSCI Emerging Markets Index over the long term by taking many small overweight and
underweight positions, resulting in low tracking error. The portfolio holds around 400 emerging market stocks, overweighting those with attractive valuations,
strong profitability, solid momentum, and recent positive analyst revisions. Using our integrated multi-factor stock selection model, we expect consistent
outperformance. The fund also seeks a better sustainability profile than the benchmark, in line with Article 8 of the SFDR. It integrates sustainability into the
investment process by applying the Robeco Level 2 exclusion list, excluding stocks with an SDG score of -3, targeting a 5% better ESG risk rating
(Sustainalytics), and aiming for 20% better carbon, waste, and water footprints than the index.
Top 10 largest holdings
The top ten positions are primarily the result of the fact that these companies have a large weight in the index.
Sector allocation
The fund aims to keep sector positions neutral to the index level. In terms of limits, +/- 0.3% over/underweights for countries and +/- 1% for sectors are
allowed versus the MSCI Emerging Markets Index.
Country / Region allocation
The fund aims to keep country positions neutral to the index level. In terms of limits, +/- 0.3% over/underweights for countries and +/- 1% for sectors are
allowed versus the MSCI Emerging Markets Index.
