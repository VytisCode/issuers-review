About this fund

Robeco 3D EM Equity UCITS ETF is an actively managed Sub-fund that invests in stocks of companies in emerging markets and will apply the Manager’s “3D” investment strategy, which seeks to consider risk, return and sustainability in the Sub-fund’s portfolio. The Sub-fund’s portfolio will be optimised using a quantitative process to target returns in excess of the Benchmark, sustainability characteristics better than the Benchmark and to manage risk compared to the Benchmark. The three dimensions of risk, return and sustainability are considered together in the Manager’s proprietary quantitative stock-ranking model.

Performance explanation

Per 31-10-2026
Based on transaction prices, the fund's return was -8.11%. The 3D Emerging Equity UCITS ETF aims to consistently outperform the MSCI Emerging Markets Index over the long term by taking many small overweight and underweight positions, resulting in low tracking error. The portfolio holds around 400 emerging market stocks, overweighting those with attractive valuations, strong profitability, solid momentum, and recent positive analyst revisions. Using our integrated multi-factor stock selection model, we expect consistent outperformance. The fund also seeks a better sustainability profile than the benchmark, in line with Article 8 of the SFDR. It integrates sustainability into the investment process by applying the Robeco Level 2 exclusion list, excluding stocks with an SDG score of -3, targeting a 5% better ESG risk rating (Sustainalytics), and aiming for 20% better carbon, waste, and water footprints than the index.
