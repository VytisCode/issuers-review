                                                                                                                                                                              LATRAPS’ sustainability development direction


                                                                       LATRAPS is building a sustainable, competitive and future-oriented agricultural ecosystem in which economic growth, environmental protection and social responsibility are closely interconnected,
                                                                       while good governance strengthens the cooperative model as a foundation for sustainable growth.

                                                                                                                                                                                                 Cooperation

                                                                       The cooperative model is at the heart of LATRAPS operations, providing its members with equal opportunities to engage with the global market. Bringing farmers together within a unified system
                                                                       strengthens their market position, facilitates knowledge sharing and creates greater resilience to external economic and climate-related risks.
