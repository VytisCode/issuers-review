                                                                                                                          General Principles for Preparing the Sustainability Report




    1 General Principles for Preparing
    the Sustainability Report
    The sustainability report of AS Tallinna Sadam (hereinafter also Tallinna Sadam, the Company or the
    Group) has been prepared for the financial year covering the period from 1 January to 31 December
    2025. The report reflects the Group's sustainability-related activities, performance, and impacts
    in the environmental, social, and governance areas (hereinafter also ESG) during the reporting period.

    The sustainability report has not been prepared to the mandatory extent of the requirements
    of the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive CSRD1 of the European Union, nor does it fully
    comply with the requirements of the European Sustainability Reporting Standards ESRS2. However,
    the report has been prepared following the principles of the CSRD and ESRS and is based on the
    framework set out in the ESRS. Due to the legal status of the Company and the applicable reporting
    requirements, not all disclosure requirements arising from the directive and standards are applicable
    and mandatory for Tallinna Sadam; therefore, this report does not include all data points and disclosure
    obligations defined in the ESRSs.

    The report includes the 2025 consolidated sustainability data of Tallinna Sadam's subsidiaries. Data
    for the associate Green Marine AS has not been included in the sustainability report, as Tallinna Sadam
    lacks a controlling influence over the company, despite a 51% shareholding.

    In assessing sustainability activities and impacts, in addition to the indicators set out in the ESRSs,
    company-specific metrics, developed to monitor and evaluate the achievement of Tallinna Sadam's
    strategic environmental, social and business objectives, are also used.

    The sustainability report has been prepared voluntarily with the aim of increasing transparency, sup-
    porting responsible and sustainable management and meeting the expectations of the owner and
    stakeholders.

    The sustainability report primarily covers topics and data points that, as a result of the double ma-
    teriality assessment, have been assessed as material to Tallinna Sadam. Additionally, certain topics
    whose impact was not assessed as material in the double materiality assessment, but which Tallinna
    Sadam considers important for informing stakeholders and ensuring a comprehensive overview, have
    also been disclosed.

     6.2 E1 Climate change
     6.2.1 Material impacts, risks, and opportunities
     As negative impacts, the company's activities affect the climate primarily through greenhouse gas
     (GHG) emissions related to shipping. Additional impacts include exhaust gases from vehicles moving
     within the harbour area and atmospheric air pollution arising from the use of fossil-based thermal
     energy. The use of non-renewable energy sources in the value chain also has a negative impact.

     As a positive impact, Tallinna Sadam uses and produces renewable electricity in its operations
     (solar panels, seawater heating and cooling), which supports the mitigation of climate impacts
     in line with sustainable development goals.

     The development of onshore power supply (OPS) and alternative fuels offers an opportunity
     to move towards carbon-free, innovative, and forward-looking business models, reduce emissions
     from vessels stationary at the port, and strengthen the company's competitiveness in an economic
     environment moving towards climate neutrality.

     As a risk, however, this same development requires significant investment volumes, the readiness
     of technical solutions, and compliance with regulatory requirements, which may increase costs
     or slow down the implementation of planned activities.

     Climate-related impacts, risks, and opportunities directly affect Tallinna Sadam's business model
     and strategic choices, as the competitiveness and sustainability of operations of harbours depend
     on energy efficiency, compliance with stricter environmental requirements, and the ability to move
     towards climate-neutral service provision. Therefore, climate-related activities and the transition
     plan are closely linked to the company's long-term strategy and investment decisions.

     8 G Governance Information
     Governance model and administrative, management, and supervisory bodies
     The Management Bodies of Tallinna Sadam are the General Meeting of Shareholders,
     the Supervisory Board, and the Management Board. In their activities, the Supervisory Board
     and the Management Board are guided by the company's strategy, values, current legislation,
     and the principles of the Good Corporate Governance Code. The objective of the management
     system is to ensure transparent, reliable, and sustainability-oriented corporate governance.


     The role of the Supervisory Board in sustainability management
     The Supervisory Board directs the strategic management of the Group, confirms targets and devel-
     opment trends, and exercises supervision over the activities of the Management Board. As part of
     its work plan, the Supervisory Board addresses risks related to the Group's core activities and focus
     themes arising from regulations, including sustainability and climate-related factors, and monitors
     the implementation of strategic projects. At least half of the members of the Supervisory Board
     (at 31 December 2025, 5/7, including the chairman of the Management Board) are independent,
     which supports the objectivity and balance of supervision.


     The role of the Management Board in sustainability management
     The Management Board is responsible for the implementation of the company's strategy,
     day-to-day management, and the fulfilment of sustainability and climate-related targets.
     The Management Board regularly provides the Supervisory Board with overviews of the Group's
     performance indicators, risks, safety, personnel and working conditions, environmental impacts,
     and other sustainability aspects important for strategic and investment decisions. Sustainability
     information is presented to the Supervisory Board together with financial and risk management
     reports.

     To ensure the sustainability competence of the management bodies, regular overviews of the
     company's sustainability and climate-related targets, risks, and major regulatory developments
     are presented to the Supervisory Board and the Management Board. If necessary, the competence
     of the management bodies is supported with additional information to ensure that sustainability
     topics are considered in the company's strategy and activities. The chairman of the Management
     Board is a member of the Supervisory Board of the Green Tiger, a cooperation platform for envi-
     ronmentally conscious companies, contributing more broadly to the implementation of sustainable
     goals to increase social awareness of green changes and strive for a balanced economic model.

     8.1.9 Payment practices and tax conduct
     In its business activities, Tallinna Sadam follows good payment practices and considers timely and
     correct settlement with its suppliers important. Invoices are paid to suppliers within the deadlines
     agreed in contracts or on invoices, and unjustified payment delays are avoided. Payments are made
